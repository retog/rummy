How to play

Goal: put as many tiles from your rack on the table as possible. Every set on the table has to stay valid.

4567

Run: 3 or more consecutive numbers of one color.

999

Group: 3 or 4 tiles of the same number in different colors.

Colors: every color has its own symbol, so you can tell the tiles apart without relying on color.

  • 5red: circle
  • 5blue: square
  • 5orange: triangle
  • 5black: star
  • Drag tiles between sets, onto New set or back to your rack. You can also tap a tile and then tap where it should go.
  • You may rearrange the table freely, but tiles from the table can't go to your rack.
  • A joker can stand for any tile.
  • Press Check when you're done. You'll learn whether your solution is optimal or whether a better one exists.
  • Invalid checks and extra tries cost 20 % of the points each. Medium puzzles count double, hard ones triple.
